BalanceAudit

class BalanceAudit

Finds records that versions before 7.1.1 may have corrupted.

Nothing here changes data. An over cleared Transaction is a fact, but how to put it right is an accounting decision. A compounded Balance cannot be told apart from one that was legitimately re-entered, so those findings are candidates to check against the source of the Balance.
